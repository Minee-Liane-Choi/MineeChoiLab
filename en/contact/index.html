Postdoctoral position: InnoCORE Fellow (AI-NAMs)

Posted 1 July 2026

The Minee Choi lab at KAIST is recruiting an InnoCORE Fellow for the AI-NAMs programme. We are looking for an AI specialist to build an organoid data bank and to develop a Cellular World Model based on time-series data.

  • Salary: KRW 90 million per year, for up to 5 years
  • Administrative and application enquiries: young9366@kaist.ac.kr
  • Programme enquiries: minee.choi@kaist.ac.kr

Graduate admissions: integrated M.S./Ph.D. program, Spring and Fall 2027

Departments: Brain and Cognitive Sciences · Graduate School of Stem Cell and Regenerative Biology

For Spring 2027, the BRØ1 lab is looking for students who bring together thinking and practical skills in AI and neuroscience, to lead a preclinical digital-twin platform for brain disease based on patient organoids. We welcome creative, self-motivated applicants who want to help transform precision medicine and drug development with patient stem cells and organoids.
