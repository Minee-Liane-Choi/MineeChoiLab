Brain organoids in culture

Research

We differentiate patient-derived induced pluripotent stem cells (hiPSCs) into brain cells and brain organoids, profile the pathophysiology of each patient, and combine these data with AI to build a preclinical testbed that predicts personalised treatments.

Tech One

Patient-derived stem cells

We differentiate patient hiPSCs into neurons to reproduce disease at the level of brain cells.

 Tech Two

Patient brain organoids

We use brain organoids grown from patient cells to study the structure and diseases of the human brain.

 Tech Three

Organoid intelligence

We connect brain organoids to computing systems to test how drugs affect cognitive function.

 Tech Four

Digital brain-cell models

AI models trained on brain-cell pathology predict how each patient will respond to drugs.
